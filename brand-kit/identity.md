Keep the majority of each composition dark. Cyan creates the leading edge of light; violet and magenta provide depth. Use gradients on focal points, not every surface. Do not use glow to compensate for weak contrast.

## Typography
Website: Arial/Helvetica with a system sans-serif fallback; Tahoma/Arial for Arabic. Display type is bold, geometric and tightly spaced in English; Arabic uses natural spacing and increased line height. Body copy has generous leading. Raster/SVG export typography uses DejaVu Sans for reliable offline rendering.

Desktop hero: approximately 68–106px; mobile: 58–73px. Section headings: 34–54px. Body: 13–16px. Social titles: 79–125px on 1080px artboards. Keep social copy concise.

## Logo
The mark pairs an architectural P with an angular W and a diagonal orbit. The dimensional PNG follows the supplied reference. The flat SVG is a simplified geometric recreation for production use at smaller sizes. These are complementary versions, not pixel-identical formats.

Use a minimum clear space of about one-quarter of the mark's width. For tiny uses, prefer the standalone mark. Use the monochrome version when gradients are unsuitable. Do not distort the proportions, add coding brackets, remove the orbit or substitute letters from a font.

## Visual rules
- Cinematic planetary scale, dark rock, detailed illuminated networks and sparse stars.
- Cyan leading edges, violet backlights and selective magenta flares.
- Glass is a supporting material, not the entire interface.
- Interface panels and devices share the same light direction and perspective.
- Large typography and clear CTA hierarchy balance detailed imagery.
- No fake customers, counts, testimonials, awards or client marks.
- Label portfolio concepts visibly.

## Motion
Use slow drift and precise transitions. Reveals move about 28px over 900ms. Buttons use a small lift. Cards tilt only a few degrees. The process uses a sticky introduction and scroll-active stages. Native scrolling is preserved. Respect reduced motion and avoid custom cursors that hide the system pointer.
